About Harish Electricals

A Trusted Name in Electrical Solutions.

Harish Electricals is a complete electrical products and home appliances store on Railway Road in Ganaur, near the New Grain Market. For 15 years we have helped families, shopkeepers, electricians and builders find the right product for the job.

Our focus is simple: quality products from recognised brands, helpful and honest customer service, and practical electrical solutions for everyday needs, from a single bulb holder to the wiring of a whole home.
